\section{AI Usage Declaration}\label{app:ai}

In accordance with the Academic Integrity policy of IM1031 -- Entrepreneurship and
Innovation, we state which AI tools were used and how. Members used AI to find references,
generate ideas, check grammar, review code and calculations, and improve presentation. In
addition, Claude was used to draft the text, figures, and typesetting of several sections and
appendices (Sections 1, 3, 4, and 5 and Appendices B--D) from the group's own earlier notes and
MVP code, and one concept image was generated with an AI image tool. Members reviewed and edited
this material, and every figure and source taken from an AI-assisted search was checked against
its original document.
