\section{AI Usage Declaration}\label{app:ai}

In accordance with the Academic Integrity policy of IM1031 -- Entrepreneurship and
Innovation, our group used AI tools only for supportive purposes: finding
references, generating ideas, checking grammar, and improving presentation. The
report was not wholly or substantially generated by AI, and every figure and
source taken from an AI-assisted search was checked against its original document.

prompt, and the purpose it served.
